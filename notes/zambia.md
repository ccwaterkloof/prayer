# Strategic prayer

- Praise God for the spread of the Gospel in Zambia. Zambians widely accept Christianity,
  even in public institutions and the media. Evangelicals grew from 3.8% in 1960 to more
  than 25% today. Former president Chiluba declared Zambia a Christian nation in 1991, and
  Zambians practise freedom of all religions. Zambia enjoys a legacy as a stable and
  peaceful nation surrounded by countries troubled by war and unrest.
- Pray for national transformation along biblical lines to come. Too few know the Word of
  God, or how to live a Christian life. Pray for Christianity to become a living faith
  rather than a cultural identity and that Zambia will truly be a nation that honours God.
- Pray for Christians to live pure and holy lives that depend on God alone for spiritual
  power and blessing.
- Pray for leadership training that raises up pastors committed to biblical teaching.
- Pray for REACH missionaries Michael & Renée Watson as they
  [settle in Mongu](https://watsonsmissions.com/the-long-road-to-mongu/)
